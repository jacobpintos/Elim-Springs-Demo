# Annual Notice of Rights — DRAFT

> **Draft template — not legal advice.** FERPA legally binds schools that receive
> U.S. Dept. of Education funding; if that isn't your school, you may adapt this
> as a good-practice notice or to meet **state** requirements. Replace bracketed
> placeholders and have counsel review before distributing. Provide this to
> parents/guardians (and eligible students 18+) **annually**.

**[School Name]** informs parents/guardians and eligible students of their rights
regarding the student's education records:

## 1. The right to inspect and review the student's education records
You may request to review the records within **45 days** of the day the school
receives the request. Submit a written request to **[records custodian, title]**
identifying the records you wish to see; we will arrange access and notify you of
the time and place.

## 2. The right to request amendment of records you believe are inaccurate
Submit a written request to **[records custodian]** identifying the record and
explaining why it is inaccurate or misleading. If we decline to amend it, we will
notify you of your right to a hearing.

## 3. The right to consent to disclosures of personally identifiable information
Your written consent is required before we disclose personally identifiable
information from the student's records, **except** to the extent that the
applicable law permits disclosure without consent — for example, to school
officials with a legitimate educational interest.

- A **school official** includes a teacher or administrator the school has
  designated, and a **service provider** (such as our gradebook/cloud host)
  performing a service for which the school would otherwise use its own
  employees, that is under the school's direct control regarding the use and
  maintenance of records.
- A **legitimate educational interest** exists when the official needs to review
  a record to fulfill their responsibilities.

## 4. Directory information
The school may designate the following as **directory information**, which may be
disclosed without consent unless you opt out: **[e.g., student name, grade level,
participation in activities, dates of attendance]**. To opt out, notify
**[records custodian]** in writing by **[date/deadline]**.

## 5. The right to file a complaint
If you believe the school has not complied with applicable requirements, you may
contact the school at **[contact]** [and, where FERPA applies, the U.S. Department
of Education's Student Privacy Policy Office].
