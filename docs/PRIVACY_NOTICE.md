**[School Name] ("we," "the school")** uses an online gradebook to keep student
records and communicate with families. This notice explains what we collect,
how we use and protect it, and your choices.

## What we collect

- **Student information:** name, grade level, and academic records — grades and
  assignments, attendance, behavior notes, and teacher observations.
- **Family/contact information:** parent/guardian name, email, and phone number.
- **Account information:** the email address used to sign in. Passwords are held
  only by our authentication provider (Google Firebase) and are never visible to
  the school.

We collect only what we need to run the school's academic program.

## How we use it

To record and report academic progress, track attendance and compliance, manage
school events and permission slips, and communicate with parents/guardians. We
do **not** sell student information or use it for advertising.

## Where it is stored

Records are stored using **Google Firebase / Google Cloud** (data centers in the
United States) under Google's data-processing terms. Data is encrypted in transit
and at rest. Access is restricted so that **a parent/guardian can see only their
own child's records**, and staff access is limited to their role and logged.

## Who can see it

- **Parents/guardians:** their own children's records, through the family portal.
- **Teachers and administrators:** records needed for their role; access is
  recorded in an internal activity log.
- **Service provider:** Google, solely to host the service on our behalf.
- **Others:** only with your written consent, or where permitted/required by law.

## How we protect it

Individual logins, strong-password requirements, optional two-factor
authentication, automatic sign-out on idle devices, per-family access
restrictions, an access/change log, and daily backups.

## Your choices and rights

- **Review:** you may request to see your child's records. We will respond within
  a reasonable time (and within any period required by law).
- **Corrections:** you may ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate.
- **Directory information / opt-out:** see the school's annual notice.
- **Questions or concerns:** contact **[Name, title]** at **[email / phone]**.

## Data retention

We keep student records for **[retention period, e.g., as required by state law]**
and securely delete them after that period.
